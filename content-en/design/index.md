## What does design mean?

Systems design is not only drawing diagrams. I think it is first of all a decision about **order and structure**, and only then a choice of technology: what is done before what, what the goal is, how the parts connect. The more powerful the technology, the faster it repeats bad design.

The pieces in this section are of two kinds: general views on what good design is, and specific tools.
