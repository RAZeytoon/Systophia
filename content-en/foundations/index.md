## What is this collection?

Lessons for someone who wants to learn systems analysis from the ground up: first what a system and analysis are, then how to define and analyse a need, and finally how to gather and validate it. Each lesson can be read on its own, but the order is built so that each rests on the one before. At the end of each lesson there is a "previous / next" link.

## Three chapters

**Chapter 1: concepts (lessons 01 to 08).** What systems analysis is and why it matters, the system itself, analysis, the quarrel between holism and reductionism, a critique of modernity, general system theory, the analyst's role and the systems development life cycle (SDLC). If you read only one lesson, read [[foundations/what-is-analysis|What is analysis, and why must an analyst think holistically?]]

**Chapter 2: defining and analysing requirements (lessons 08 to 15).** Why requirements matter, the difference between defining and analysing, an exercise in defining a system, assumption versus hypothesis, factors to examine, beginners' mistakes, use cases and the SRS document.

**Chapter 3: from gathering to validation (lessons 16 to 20).** An introduction to gathering, techniques for gathering, analysing and classifying requirements, validation, and finally expert systems.
