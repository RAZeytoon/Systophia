## What is this place?

Systophia is my school of thought on systems thinking and systems analysis: how systems work, how they break, and how they can be designed. The original essays are long-form and written in Persian.

This is the English edition. It is a selection, not a mirror: essays are translated one by one, and each translated page links back to its Persian original (top of the sidebar, "فارسی").
