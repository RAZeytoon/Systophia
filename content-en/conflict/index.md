> In large teams, conflict becomes constructive; the free flow of conflicting ideas is vital for creative thought. – Peter Senge, *The Fifth Discipline* (paraphrased)

## Conflict is not the problem; staying hidden is

Every real system has several stakeholders and several views, and they do not always agree: finance wants access closed and marketing wants it open. Conflict that is seen and managed can produce a new idea; conflict that is ignored becomes a crisis.

## Pieces

- [[conflict/multi-perspective-observability|Multi-perspective observability]]: each view sees only part of the system; the engineering, human and economic must be heard together.
- [[foundations/analysing-and-classifying-requirements|Analysing and classifying requirements]]: the section on "managing conflicts" shows what the analyst does when two stakeholders have opposing wants.
- [[foundations/common-mistakes-new-analysts|Common mistakes of new analysts]]: one common error is listening to only one stakeholder.
- [[foundations/holism-and-reductionism|Holism and reductionism: two views in analysis]]: the old quarrel between holism and reductionism is also a kind of conflict of views.
