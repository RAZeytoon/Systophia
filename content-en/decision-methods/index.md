> This section holds notes from courses I took during my doctorate. I rewrote the text myself and recomputed the numerical examples. To me the three courses form one thread: **how do we decide when there are several goals and several parties?**

## Three courses, one question

Systems analysis ends in a decision. When alternatives are many and criteria conflict, **multi-attribute decision making** gives tools. When more than one party is deciding and each thinks for itself, **game theory** becomes necessary. And when the decision is to build a physical network, **supply chain network design** turns the problem into a mathematical model. I think none of them decides for us; they only make the assumptions explicit and open to debate.
