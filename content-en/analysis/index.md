## Analysis means "break it apart and connect it"

Systems analysis is two jobs: breaking a problem into parts and joining them again until structure appears (for its exact meaning see [[foundations/what-is-analysis|What is analysis, and why must an analyst think holistically?]]). This section is the home of more basic questions: how we turn data into understanding, where we put theory, and how we see many-party relations.

## Pieces

- [[analysis/dikw|From data to insight: the pyramid that guides understanding (DIKW)]]: the DIKW pyramid (data, information, knowledge, wisdom), which question each level answers, and why having a report is not having insight.
- [[analysis/theory-first-or-data-first|Theory first or data first: two views of data analysis]]: two traditions in data analysis, a hypothesis in advance or a pattern that emerges from the data, and what the analyst does between them.
- [[analysis/q-analysis|The hidden geometry of "us" (Q-Analysis)]]: a mathematical method by Ronald Atkin that keeps many-party relations in one piece, with a full example.
- [[analysis/analysis-destroys-wholes|Analysing destroys wholes]]: a line from *The Bridges of Madison County* and the question of whether analysis destroys wholeness.
