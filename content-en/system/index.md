## What does "system" mean?

"System" is one of those words we all say and rarely define precisely: education system, administrative system, operating system, system of governance. What they all share is a set of parts that relate to each other and work toward a purpose. Without parts there is no system; without relations it is only a heap; and without a purpose it is an accidental collection.

This section is the home of the concept itself, before we go on to analysis or design. To me, until we know what a system is and where its boundary lies, any analysis hangs in the air.
