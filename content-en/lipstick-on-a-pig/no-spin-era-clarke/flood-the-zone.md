# Flood the Zone

<small>Term: *Flood the Zone* | Book: [[book-profile-clarke|Lipstick on a Pig (the no-spin era): book profile and conceptual summary]]</small>

## Definition and main idea

In the framework that the underlying summary attributes to the book, this title refers to an active, continuous presence in the information field with honest information and clear explanations, instead of silence, a late reaction or leaving the narrative entirely to others.

## Explanation and elaboration

An organisation can help audiences get a fuller picture by publishing relevant documents, answering regularly, keeping spokespeople available and explaining decisions clearly. Even so, a high volume of messages is not transparency in itself. Information must be relevant, checkable and honest, and must answer the hard questions too.

## Notes and limits of interpretation

> [!note] Source versus analysis
> In other political usages the English phrase "Flood the Zone" has sometimes meant saturating the public space with many, or misleading, messages. That other meaning should not be assumed to be the same as Clarke's chapter without checking. This chapter's account needs to be compared with the full text of the book.
