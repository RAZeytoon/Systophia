# It's About One Thing / Message & Objective First

<small>Term: *It's About One Thing / Message & Objective First* | Book: [[book-profile-clarke|Lipstick on a Pig (the no-spin era): book profile and conceptual summary]]</small>

## Definition and main idea

Effective communication starts with the goal and the main message, not with the wish to generate positive coverage. The choice of media, spokesperson and tactic should follow the goal and the audience.

## Explanation and elaboration

The goal specifies what change is wanted; the main message is what the audience must understand; the strategy is the logic of reaching the goal; and a tactic is a specific action such as an interview, a statement, a report or a public meeting. According to the underlying summary, Clarke stresses that "positive coverage" is not in itself a goal, and that placing a favourable article in a particular newspaper is not necessarily a strategy. The channel must fit the audience and the behaviour expected of them.

## Notes and limits of interpretation

> [!note] Source versus analysis
> The number of news items or views may show reach, but alone it does not prove that the audience's understanding, trust or behaviour has changed. Derive the indicators from the communication goal.
