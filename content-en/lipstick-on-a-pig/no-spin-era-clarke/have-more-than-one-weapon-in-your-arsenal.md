# Have More Than One Weapon in Your Arsenal

<small>Term: *Have More Than One Weapon in Your Arsenal* | Book: [[book-profile-clarke|Lipstick on a Pig (the no-spin era): book profile and conceptual summary]]</small>

## Definition and main idea

The title of the third chapter suggests that relying on a single tactic or medium is not enough for every situation; but the underlying summary gave no detailed account of this chapter's examples and rules.

## Explanation and elaboration

As a practical framework, a communication team can consider options such as a direct response to a reporter, a website and checkable documents, a public meeting, internal communication, a visual explainer, social networks, or direct contact with the target group. The choice should be based on the goal, the audience, urgency, the complexity of the message and the level of trust in the channel. This part is a practical elaboration of the chapter title, not a confirmed retelling of the author's own examples.
