# Deliver the Bad News Yourself & Admit Mistakes Fast

<small>Term: *Deliver the Bad News Yourself & Admit Mistakes Fast* | Book: [[book-profile-clarke|Lipstick on a Pig (the no-spin era): book profile and conceptual summary]]</small>

## Definition and main idea

When there is unwelcome news or an error, the organisation should announce the facts itself at the right time, accept its share of the mistake and explain what it is doing to put things right.

## Explanation and elaboration

Getting ahead with the information makes it possible to explain context, impact and corrective action, but it does not guarantee that criticism will disappear. Admitting an error must be specific: what happened, who was affected, which decision or execution was wrong, what action is being taken and how repetition will be prevented. A vague apology with no real change may look merely tactical.

## Notes and limits of interpretation

> [!note] Source versus analysis
> Getting ahead should not turn into hasty publication of wrong information or a breach of privacy. Legal and operational coordination is sometimes necessary, but it must not become an excuse for misleading concealment.
