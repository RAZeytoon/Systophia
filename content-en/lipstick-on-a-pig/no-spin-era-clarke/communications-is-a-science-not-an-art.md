# Communications Is a Science, Not an Art

<small>Term: *Communications Is a Science, Not an Art* | Book: [[book-profile-clarke|Lipstick on a Pig (the no-spin era): book profile and conceptual summary]]</small>

## Definition and main idea

The title of the sixth chapter implies that communication can be planned, tested, measured and learned from systematically; success does not depend only on inspiration or individual skill.

## Explanation and elaboration

A practical cycle might be: define the goal, understand the audience, draft the message, choose the channel, execute, monitor feedback and adjust. Data such as reach, message comprehension, quality of questions, behaviour change or trust each show one aspect of the result and should be chosen to fit the goal. This approach does not mean there is a definitive formula for all communication situations.
