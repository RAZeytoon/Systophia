# Being Alive / Self-Supporting Quality

<small>Original term: *Being Alive / Self-Supporting Quality* | Chapter/location: 3 | Chapter start page in the English edition's table of contents: 41</small>

## Definition and core idea

The quality of an environment and the quality of human experience are linked: the environment affects human presence and activity, and people in turn affect the life of the environment through use and care.

## Explanation and elaboration

A place is not just a neutral container for behaviour. Layout, boundaries, light, access and the relation of spaces make some activities easier or harder. Conversely, users' habits, events and care also change the character of a place. "Self-supporting" can be understood as a coherence that arises from inner relations, not complete independence from maintenance and resources.

## Notes and limits of interpretation

> [!note] Caution in attribution
> From an architectural example alone one cannot conclude a general causal relation between the environment and wellbeing. Users' experience, the social context, accessibility and time all matter.
