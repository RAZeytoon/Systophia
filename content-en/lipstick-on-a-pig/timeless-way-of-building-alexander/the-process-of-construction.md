# The Process of Construction

<small>Original term: *The Process of Construction* | Chapter/location: 23 | Chapter start page in the English edition's table of contents: 455</small>

## Definition and core idea

The process of execution should not be merely a mechanical copy of an abstract picture; construction can respond to the real conditions of the place and to information revealed during the work.

## Explanation and elaboration

On site, slope, light, materials, real dimensions, workmanship and discovered conditions can adjust detailed decisions. This can be read as a principle of feedback between design and construction, not an absolute abolition of the plan or model.

## Notes and limits of interpretation

> [!note] Caution in attribution
> Building directly without drawings and technical control is not safe general advice. Drawings, calculations, permits, safety and quality control must be kept.
