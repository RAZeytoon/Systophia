# Differentiating Space

<small>Original term: *Differentiating Space* | Chapter/location: 19 | Chapter start page in the English edition's table of contents: 365</small>

## Definition and core idea

The environment takes shape through successive, related changes that make the whole clearer, not merely by placing pre-designed pieces side by side. The metaphor of unfolding highlights how the parts depend on the whole.

## Explanation and elaboration

Fixing a shared space can influence paths, entrances, the orientation of rooms and the position of openings. Each decision changes the context of the next one; design becomes a string of continuous choices that can be revisited.

## Notes and limits of interpretation

> [!note] Caution in attribution
> The analogy with the growth of an embryo is a metaphor, not a biological claim about buildings. Complex projects still need engineering analysis, regulations and resource management.
