# Alive, Whole, Comfortable, Free, Exact, Egoless, Eternal

<small>Original term: *Alive, Whole, Comfortable, Free, Exact, Egoless, Eternal* | Chapter/location: 2 and 8 | Chapter start page in the English edition's table of contents: 19 and 137</small>

## Definition and core idea

Seven overlapping descriptions for approaching the quality without a name; not seven fully independent criteria and not a validated quantitative scale.

## Explanation and elaboration

- **Alive:** has dynamism and meaningful relations among parts.
- **Whole:** the parts sit in an understandable whole.
- **Comfortable:** the space allows presence without unnecessary tension.
- **Free:** the space is not overly captive to artifice and show.
- **Exact:** details respond to the place and to real use.
- **Egoless:** the designer's self-display does not override real needs.
- **Eternal:** a lasting quality that goes beyond passing fashion; not eternal physical life.

## Notes and limits of interpretation

> [!note] Caution in attribution
> The translation of the words is approximate. These seven descriptions should not be turned into a checklist or a psychometric scale without a method and evidence.
