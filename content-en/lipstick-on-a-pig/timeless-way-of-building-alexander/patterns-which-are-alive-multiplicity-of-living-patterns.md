# Patterns Which Are Alive / Multiplicity of Living Patterns

<small>Original term: *Patterns Which Are Alive / Multiplicity of Living Patterns* | Chapter/location: 6 and 7 | Chapter start page in the English edition's table of contents: 101 and 123</small>

## Definition and core idea

The quality of a place is often the result of several related patterns cooperating at different scales; so patterns have to be judged both on their own and in relation to their surroundings.

## Explanation and elaboration

A seating area may look suitable in itself while access, light, privacy or neighbouring activities are unsuitable. Choosing one local pattern affects its neighbouring patterns; design is not just sticking together a few independent solutions.

## Notes and limits of interpretation

> [!note] Caution in attribution
> That one unsuitable pattern necessarily brings down the whole system is not an empirical law without exceptions; how strong the effect is depends on the context.
