# Its Ageless Character

<small>Original term: *Its Ageless Character* | Chapter/location: 26 | Chapter start page in the English edition's table of contents: 511</small>

## Definition and core idea

A quality that is not the same as passing fashion or a superficial imitation of a historical style; in this theory it comes from the fit of relations, response to conditions and a sense of coherence.

## Explanation and elaboration

A contemporary building can create a sense of permanence and familiarity. By contrast, copying historical façades and details does not guarantee that a building suits its place or its users' needs. One can examine the durability of use, the possibility of maintenance, the relation to activities and the dependence on fashion.

## Notes and limits of interpretation

> [!note] Caution in attribution
> "Timeless" is an evaluative, interpretive concept; it should not turn into historical stasis or a uniformity of tastes.
