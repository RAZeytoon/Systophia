# The Slow Emergence of a Town

<small>Original term: *The Slow Emergence of a Town* | Chapter/location: 25 | Chapter start page in the English edition's table of contents: 493</small>

## Definition and core idea

Urban order can arise from local, successive decisions, rather than its quality being drawn only from a predetermined final picture.

## Explanation and elaboration

Decisions about the plot, the building, the path and public space affect one another. From today's viewpoint the idea can be connected with adaptive and participatory planning: a general framework exists, but the details respond to real feedback.

## Notes and limits of interpretation

> [!note] Caution in attribution
> Criticising the master plan does not mean removing all central coordination; transport, infrastructure, risk management and spatial justice need large-scale coordination. The question is the kind and degree of control.
