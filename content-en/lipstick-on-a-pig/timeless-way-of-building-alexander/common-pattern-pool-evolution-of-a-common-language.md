# Common Pattern Pool / Evolution of a Common Language

<small>Original term: *Common Pattern Pool / Evolution of a Common Language* | Chapter/location: 17 | Chapter start page in the English edition's table of contents: 325</small>

## Definition and core idea

A shared stock of spatial knowledge and solutions that forms within a community and changes through use, experience and gradual correction.

## Explanation and elaboration

Spatial knowledge is not only in books; it also lives in habits, construction, maintenance and repeated decisions. A pattern language has to allow learning: some patterns adapt to new conditions and some need to be changed or set aside.

## Notes and limits of interpretation

> [!note] Caution in attribution
> Evolution should not be seen only as the natural selection of good patterns; power, economics, regulation and inequality also affect whether a pattern survives.
