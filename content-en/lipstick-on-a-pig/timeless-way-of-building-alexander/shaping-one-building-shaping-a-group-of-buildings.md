# Shaping One Building / Shaping a Group of Buildings

<small>Original term: *Shaping One Building / Shaping a Group of Buildings* | Chapter/location: 21 and 22 | Chapter start page in the English edition's table of contents: 403 and 427</small>

## Definition and core idea

Quality at the scale of one building is not enough; the relation of buildings to one another and to the spaces between them also decides the outcome.

## Explanation and elaboration

At the scale of a building, the relation of spaces, entrances, light and activity matters. At the scale of a group of buildings, the street, courtyard, square, path, edges and distances are part of the design, not just empty space left over after buildings are placed.
