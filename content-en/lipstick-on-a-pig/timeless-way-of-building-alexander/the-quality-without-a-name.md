# The Quality Without a Name

<small>Original term: *The Quality Without a Name* | Chapter/location: 2 | Chapter start page in the English edition's table of contents: 19</small>

## Definition and core idea

A quality that is felt directly in a person, a building, a city or a landscape but which, in Alexander's view, cannot be fully defined by a single word or criterion.

## Explanation and elaboration

Alexander approaches this quality through seven overlapping descriptions: alive, whole, comfortable, free, exact, egoless and eternal. These descriptions are not independent parts to be added up mechanically; each illuminates one aspect of the overall experienced quality.

## Notes and limits of interpretation

> [!note] Caution in attribution
> That this quality is objective and exact is Alexander's claim and has been criticised in the research literature. Keep the theorist's claim, users' experience and observable indicators apart.
