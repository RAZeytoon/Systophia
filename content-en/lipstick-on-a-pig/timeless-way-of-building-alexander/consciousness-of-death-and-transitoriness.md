# Consciousness of Death and Transitoriness

<small>Original term: *Consciousness of Death and Transitoriness* | Chapter/location: exact location needs checking | Chapter start page in the English edition's table of contents: unknown</small>

## Definition and core idea

The living quality of an environment can be tied to accepting the passage of time, wear and the traces of use; but not every deterioration is beautiful or desirable.

## Explanation and elaboration

Wood fades, stone is polished, and materials take on the marks of use. Hiding every trace of time may destroy part of a place's character. Even so, meaningful wear has to be distinguished from dangerous deterioration, and safety, hygiene and accessibility must be kept.
