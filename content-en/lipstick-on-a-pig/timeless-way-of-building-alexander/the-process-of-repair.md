# The Process of Repair

<small>Original term: *The Process of Repair* | Chapter/location: 24 | Chapter start page in the English edition's table of contents: 475</small>

## Definition and core idea

A building or a town is not completed in a single moment; gradual repair, addition, correction and care can contribute to the growth and improvement of the environment.

## Explanation and elaboration

Every change to an existing environment can both solve a local problem and revisit its relation to the whole. Maintenance and upgrading are part of the cycle that forms a place. This view fits adaptive design and phased renewal.

## Notes and limits of interpretation

> [!note] Caution in attribution
> Gradual repair is not always better than new construction; safety, life-cycle cost, accessibility and structural limits may justify extensive rebuilding.
