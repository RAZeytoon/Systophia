# Pattern Language

<small>Original term: *Pattern Language* | Chapter/location: 9 to 18 | Chapter start page in the English edition's table of contents: 157 to 364</small>

## Definition and core idea

An organised set of related patterns that can formulate solutions to recurring design problems.

## Explanation and elaboration

The analogy with natural language stresses combinability: patterns provide shared rules and vocabulary, but their relations and order also matter. The "genetic code" metaphor points to the language's generative capacity; it is not an exact scientific equivalence with biological DNA.

## Notes and limits of interpretation

> [!note] Caution in attribution
> *The Timeless Way* sets out the theoretical foundations; *A Pattern Language* presents a detailed, applied collection of patterns. The two are related but are not a single work.
