# Club Goods / Free Riders

<small>Term: *Club Goods / Free Riders* | Book: [[book-profile-deane-johns|Lipstick on a Pig (financial bailouts): book profile and conceptual summary]]</small>

## Definition and core idea

Club goods in this context refers to the benefits and services that membership of a group or network brings. A "free rider" is someone who enjoys a shared benefit without contributing a proportionate share of the costs or collective effort.

## Explanation and elaboration

Civic networks and interest groups need trust, participation, resources and clear rules to continue. If members pass the cost on to others or ignore the rules, cooperation may weaken. On the other hand, rules that are too strict can push out people with few resources.

This concept helps us ask how participation is encouraged, how obligations are divided, and what mechanism exists for resolving conflict or preventing abuse.

## Notes and limits of interpretation

> [!note] Separating claim from fact
> "Club good" has a technical meaning in economics and is not the same as "public good"; the details of the author's use of the term need to be checked against the text of the relevant chapter.
