# Broader Value Beyond GDP

<small>Term: *Broader Value Beyond GDP* | Book: [[book-profile-deane-johns|Lipstick on a Pig (financial bailouts): book profile and conceptual summary]]</small>

## Definition and core idea

This concept stresses that social value cannot be measured by gross domestic product or market transactions alone; voluntary work, care, mutual aid, social trust and community cohesion can also matter for wellbeing.

## Explanation and elaboration

In the logic of the underlying summary, people power and civil institutions produce, besides financially measurable results, values that conventional economic indicators capture poorly. A thorough analysis has to distinguish between direct financial value, indirect social effects and normative claims.

To keep the concept from going vague, we have to say which outcome we mean: more trust, resilience in a crisis, access to services, less isolation, or capacity for collective action. Some of these dimensions can be measured with indicators, but turning all values into a single monetary figure is not always appropriate.

## Notes and limits of interpretation

> [!note] Separating claim from fact
> Claiming "huge value", or giving a precise size for non-financial value, requires a measurement method and data. This concept alone does not prove that every voluntary action has a positive or measurable effect.
