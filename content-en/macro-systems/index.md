## The level above the organisation

Most analyses stay at the level of one organisation or one project. A macro system is the level where **cultural values, customs and laws** take effect; that is, the setting of all smaller systems. I think an analyst who does not see this level sometimes gives a technical solution to a problem whose root is cultural or civilisational.

For now this section is filled with pieces that look from this angle:

- [[foundations/critique-of-modernity|A critique of modernity and a civilisational view of systems]]: Spengler and *The Decline of the West*, and the question of whether people exist for civilisation or civilisation for people.
- [[essays/selling-your-location-without-your-ideals|Without keeping your ideals, selling your location ends in shame]]: when income decides the ideals; from transit corridors to culture.
- [[crisis/from-order-to-the-appearance-of-order|From order to the appearance of order]]: worn-out institutions and values, and "theatrical order".
- [[crisis/grassroots-institutions-and-declining-social-responsibility|Grassroots institutions and the decline of the sense of social responsibility]]: when helping others moves from an individual duty to an institutional one.
