## When a system meets reality

Systems look complete and flawless until they meet reality. A crisis is the moment their hidden assumptions come into the open. The two pieces in this section see crisis from the angle of **institution and meaning**: when an institution that was meant to give order or support loses trust, what is left?

## Pieces

- [[crisis/from-order-to-the-appearance-of-order|From order to the appearance of order]]: three kinds of institution (inherent-communal, state-supportive, order-keeping), the crisis of legitimacy and meaning, and whether worn-out institutions are scaffolding or a prison.
- [[crisis/grassroots-institutions-and-declining-social-responsibility|Grassroots institutions and the decline of the sense of social responsibility]]: when social responsibility moves from the people's shoulders to an institution, what becomes of the sense of duty?
